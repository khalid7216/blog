how to make sandwhich

  1. Boil the chicken until fully cooked, then shred it into small pieces.
  2. In a bowl, mix the shredded chicken with mayonnaise and black pepper.
  3. Add a pinch of salt or spices as desired and mix well.
  4. Take two slices of bread and lightly toast them if preferred.
  5. Spread the chicken mixture evenly on one slice of the bread.
  6. Add chopped capsicum and lettuce on top of the chicken mix.
  7. Cover it with the other slice of bread to make a sandwich.
  8. Cut into halves or serve whole with ketchup or green chutney.
